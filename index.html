How this video trimmer works

Your browser opens the file directly from your device. When you press Trim & download, it plays the part you selected, records it with the browser's built-in recorder (MediaRecorder) and saves that recording as a new file.

  • ✓Recording runs in real time: a 30-second clip takes about 30 seconds. Keep this tab open and visible until the download starts.
  • ✓Most browsers save the clip as WebM (MP4 where the browser records MP4). It is re-encoded, so quality and file size can differ from the original.
  • ✓Long or high-resolution videos need a lot of memory. Close other tabs if the page slows down.

❓ Frequently asked questions

Q. Is my video uploaded to a server? ▼
No. The file stays on your device. The page reads it with your browser's File API and nothing is sent to our server.
Q. Does the clip get a watermark? ▼
No. No logo or watermark is added to the clip.
Q. Is the clip the same quality as the original? ▼
Not exactly. The selected part is re-recorded in real time, so it is re-encoded (usually as WebM) and quality and file size can differ from the original. For a lossless cut, use a desktop editor that can copy the original stream.
Q. How exact is the cut? ▼
The sliders move in 0.1-second steps. Because the clip is recorded while it plays, it can start or end a fraction of a second away from the times you chose.
Note: Speed depends on your device and browser. Only trim and share videos you have the right to use.
